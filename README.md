🚀 ASTRA

Autonomous Space Telemetry, Risk Intelligence & Astronaut Health Analytics

Protecting Human Life Beyond Earth Through Intelligent Health Systems.

🌌 Overview

ASTRA is an AI-powered astronaut health monitoring and decision-support platform designed for space missions.

The system continuously analyses simulated physiological telemetry, identifies abnormal health patterns, estimates potential health risks and supports autonomous onboard monitoring.

ASTRA is designed to demonstrate health intelligence even during communication interruptions between spacecraft and Earth.

🎯 Core Objectives

- Continuous astronaut health monitoring.
- AI-assisted anomaly detection.
- Predictive health risk assessment.
- Astronaut Digital Twin.
- Autonomous emergency workflows.
- Communication blackout resilience.
- Explainable AI health analytics.
- Mission health history and reporting.

⚠️ Disclaimer

ASTRA is an experimental hackathon prototype. It is not a medically validated or flight-certified system. Simulated readings and risk indicators must not be interpreted as medical diagnoses.
