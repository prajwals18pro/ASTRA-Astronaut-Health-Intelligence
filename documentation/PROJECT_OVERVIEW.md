ASTRA — Project Overview

1. Mission

Develop an intelligent astronaut health-monitoring and decision-support platform for long-duration space missions.

2. Problem

Astronauts experience unique physiological and operational challenges during spaceflight. Communication delays and limited onboard medical resources make continuous health monitoring essential.

3. Proposed Solution

ASTRA combines simulated physiological telemetry, AI-assisted anomaly detection, predictive analytics and autonomous emergency workflows.

4. Core Innovations

- Astronaut Digital Twin
- AI Health Intelligence
- Predictive Health Analytics
- Explainable AI
- Autonomous Emergency Response
- Communication Blackout Resilience

5. Development Approach

The prototype will use simulated astronaut telemetry and software-based health analytics.

6. Expected Outcome

An interactive mission-control platform demonstrating continuous health monitoring and onboard decision support.
